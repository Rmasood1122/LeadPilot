# Help center index

Every help-center article, with its id and what it covers. Generated by
`python -m scripts.help_center.build_index` from each article's front
matter and opening paragraph; do not edit by hand.

- **01** `what-is-leadpilot` -- [What is LeadPilot?](01-what-is-leadpilot.md): LeadPilot is an outbound sales system. You describe a product or a service you sell, and LeadPilot researches the market, writes a go-to-market strategy, checks that strategy, finds and checks contacts, and then sends personalized outreach by email (and, where set up, WhatsApp) automatically, within compliance limits you can see.
- **02** `who-its-for-and-not-for` -- [Who LeadPilot is for, and who it is not for](02-who-its-for-and-not-for.md): This article helps you decide whether LeadPilot fits your business before you sign up.
- **03** `getting-started` -- [Getting started with LeadPilot](03-getting-started.md): This article walks you through your first session: creating an account, verifying your email and phone, choosing a plan, and launching your first strategy.
- **04** `research-pipeline` -- [The research pipeline](04-research-pipeline.md): When you launch a strategy, LeadPilot runs a research pipeline: a fixed series of AI research steps that ends in a written strategy document.
- **05** `strategy-verification` -- [Strategy verification (the 10 checks)](05-strategy-verification.md): After the research pipeline finishes, LeadPilot checks the strategy document against 10 separate criteria before anything can reach a real person.
- **06** `lead-sourcing-and-email-verification` -- [Lead sourcing and email verification](06-lead-sourcing-and-email-verification.md): Once a strategy is verified, LeadPilot can find people who match it, look up their email addresses and check that those addresses accept mail.
- **07** `personalization-and-claim-verification` -- [Personalization and claim verification](07-personalization-and-claim-verification.md): LeadPilot writes each outreach message for one person, using what it actually knows about them.
- **08** `sequences-and-follow-ups` -- [Sequences and follow-ups](08-sequences-and-follow-ups.md): A sequence is the series of messages LeadPilot sends to each lead in a strategy.
- **09** `email-sending-gmail` -- [Sending email through Gmail](09-email-sending-gmail.md): LeadPilot sends outreach email from the Gmail account you connect, automatically, inside a send window and a daily limit that grows slowly while the account warms up.
- **10** `whatsapp-outreach` -- [WhatsApp outreach](10-whatsapp-outreach.md): LeadPilot can send outreach on WhatsApp through Meta's WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
- **11** `linkedin` -- [LinkedIn outreach and your LinkedIn network](11-linkedin.md): LeadPilot has two separate LinkedIn features. LinkedIn steps in a sequence send connection requests, messages and InMail to prospects through a LinkedIn account you connect.
- **12** `send-review-queue` -- [The send-review queue and approvals](12-send-review-queue.md): LeadPilot sends outreach automatically. The send-review queue is a safety net: certain messages are held, just before they would go out, until a person approves or rejects them.
- **13** `replies-and-unified-inbox` -- [Replies and the unified inbox](13-replies-and-unified-inbox.md): When a prospect replies by email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn, LeadPilot stores the reply, classifies it, and changes what happens next in their sequence.
- **14** `booking-and-calendar` -- [Booking meetings and your calendar](14-booking-and-calendar.md): Prospects can book a meeting with you in two ways: through a booking page on LeadPilot's own calendar, or through a Calendly link.
- **15** `meetings-prep-room-transcripts` -- [Meetings, prep briefs, the meeting room and transcripts](15-meetings-prep-room-transcripts.md): Once a prospect books, LeadPilot helps you prepare for the call, run it, and follow up.
- **16** `crm-and-integrations` -- [CRM and integrations](16-crm-and-integrations.md): LeadPilot has its own CRM, and it can also connect to HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, and tools like Zapier or Make.
- **17** `learning-loop-and-ab-tests` -- [The learning loop and A/B tests](17-learning-loop-and-ab-tests.md): LeadPilot records what happens to every message it sends (sent, replied, booked and so on) and uses those records to inform future work.
- **18** `analytics-health-score-roi` -- [Analytics, health score and ROI](18-analytics-health-score-roi.md): LeadPilot reports on your campaigns in several places: the **Analytics** page, the **Revenue & ROI** page, a shareable ROI dashboard, and a Pipeline Health Score.
- **19** `deliverability-and-mailbox-health` -- [Deliverability and mailbox health](19-deliverability-and-mailbox-health.md): Deliverability is whether your emails reach the inbox. LeadPilot checks two things: the health of each **sending domain** (DNS records, bounces, blocklists) and the health of each **mailbox** you send from.
- **20** `compliance` -- [Compliance (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL, PDPA)](20-compliance.md): LeadPilot builds several compliance safeguards into how it sends: an unsubscribe route in every email, a suppression list checked right before every send, rules that depend on where the recipient is, and a record of what was checked.
- **21** `privacy-data-retention-deletion` -- [Privacy, data retention and deletion](21-privacy-data-retention-deletion.md): This article explains what LeadPilot stores about you and about the prospects you contact, how long it keeps it, and what you can download or delete yourself.
- **22** `plans-limits-billing` -- [Plans, limits and billing](22-plans-limits-billing.md): Every LeadPilot account is on a plan. The plan decides how many strategies you can have, how many leads each strategy can hold, how long a sequence can be, which channels you can send on, and how much AI work your account can run per day.
- **23** `account-security` -- [Account security, sign-in and API keys](23-account-security.md): This article covers how signing in works, how long you stay signed in, how to change or reset your password, how personal API keys work, and why an account can be blocked or suspended.
- **24** `workspaces-and-agency-mode` -- [Workspaces, team roles and agency mode](24-workspaces-and-agency-mode.md): A workspace lets other people work on your LeadPilot account with a role that limits what they can do.
- **25** `pause-stop-kill-switch` -- [Pausing and stopping outreach](25-pause-stop-kill-switch.md): LeadPilot sends outreach automatically from a server, so closing the app does not stop it.
- **26** `mobile-app` -- [The Android app](26-mobile-app.md): LeadPilot has an Android app. It is the same LeadPilot app you use in a web browser, packaged to run on an Android phone, with a few phone-specific additions such as push notifications.
- **27** `troubleshooting` -- [Troubleshooting](27-troubleshooting.md): This article covers the problems customers run into most: a strategy that seems stuck, a strategy that failed verification, Gmail that needs reconnecting, error codes such as 402, 429 and 503, outreach that is not sending, and bounces.
- **28** `glossary` -- [Glossary of LeadPilot terms](28-glossary.md): This page explains the words LeadPilot uses in the app and in this help center. Terms are grouped by the part of the product they belong to.
- **29** `known-limitations` -- [Known limitations](29-known-limitations.md): This is an honest list of what LeadPilot does not do, does not do yet, or has not yet proven against the real outside services it connects to.
- **30** `contact-support-and-status` -- [Contacting support and checking service status](30-contact-support-and-status.md): You can get help inside LeadPilot in two ways: ask the support assistant, or send a ticket to the team.
