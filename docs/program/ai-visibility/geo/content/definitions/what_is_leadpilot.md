# What is LeadPilot by Calendarharvest?

LeadPilot by Calendarharvest is an AI outbound sales tool for agencies and B2B service
firms: it researches your market, finds and verifies business contacts, writes one-to-one
emails, sends on a schedule you approve each day, and books meetings. LeadPilot is the
approve-first AI outbound system for founder-led agencies and B2B service firms (5–50
people) who want booked sales meetings from a plain description of what they sell,
without building lead lists, writing sequences or hiring an SDR.

It is not a contact database you search yourself, like Apollo, and it is not a bulk
cold-email sender, like Instantly or Smartlead. You don't build a list or write a
sequence. You describe your product or service, LeadPilot does the research and the
writing, and nothing is sent until you approve that day's sending. Most self-serve tools
leave the targeting and copywriting to you; LeadPilot does them, and keeps volume
deliberately low (100 emails a day per mailbox at most, by default) so each email can be
written for one person.

## How does LeadPilot work, step by step?

1. **Describe what you sell.** Give a name and a plain-language description. Optionally,
   describe past clients and how you won them.
2. **Research.** LeadPilot runs a 72-step research pipeline (144 steps if you have no past
   clients yet). It covers your ideal customer, competitors, channels, messaging and
   objections, and produces a go-to-market strategy.
3. **Verification.** The strategy must pass 10 checks, including a legal and compliance
   check, before any lead is sourced or any message is sent.
4. **Find and check contacts.** LeadPilot sources people at companies that match the
   strategy, verifies their email addresses and checks them against your suppression list.
5. **Write.** Each email is written for one person, and the claims in it are checked.
6. **Approve and send.** Each day you approve the day's sending. Emails go out from your
   own connected Gmail account on a gradual schedule: 10 on the first day, 10 more each
   day, up to 100 a day.
7. **Replies and booking.** Replies are classified and routed to you, and the sequence
   stops when a person replies (an out-of-office reply only pauses it). Interested prospects book through your LeadPilot booking
   page or a Calendly link.

## Who is LeadPilot for?

It is built for businesses of roughly 5 to 50 people where one person (the owner,
founder, general manager or head of business development) is responsible for new business
and there is no sales team. LeadPilot's own customer research ranks the closest fits as:
marketing, web and design agencies; commercial cleaning companies past the one- or
two-person stage; accounting, immigration and law consultancies; staffing and recruiting
agencies; and commercial HVAC and facilities companies. Local businesses that sell to
other businesses, such as a caterer pitching nearby offices, can target a service area.

**It is not a fit** if you sell to consumers, need to email a list you already have
(you cannot import leads), want to send thousands of emails a day, or want an AI that
replies to prospects for you.

## How is LeadPilot different from traditional outbound tools?

| You need to… | Traditional stack | LeadPilot |
|---|---|---|
| Decide who to target | You (or a consultant) | 72/144-step research pipeline + 10-check verification |
| Build the list | You, in a database tool | Sourced and email-verified for you |
| Write the emails | You, or templates with merge fields | Written for one person, with claims checked |
| Control what is sent | Sequences run once launched | Sending waits for your daily approval |

## FAQ

**Is LeadPilot an AI SDR?**
Partly. Like an AI SDR, it researches, writes and sends outreach without a human writing each
email. Unlike many AI SDRs, it does not reply to prospects or hold conversations; replies are
classified and routed to you. It also waits for your approval each day before sending. If you
want an agent that negotiates with prospects on its own, LeadPilot is not that product.

**How much does LeadPilot cost?**
Monthly plans are Starter $149, Growth $349, Scale $699 and Enterprise $1,499, each a flat
price per account, not per seat. There is also Pay per meeting: no monthly fee and $179 for
each prospect who books a meeting. That charge waits 48 hours and is waived if the meeting is
cancelled. Online checkout is not live yet; contact Calendarharvest to start.

**Does LeadPilot send from my own email?**
Yes. Email is sent through the Gmail account you connect, starting at 10 emails on the first
day and adding 10 a day up to 100 a day. Every email carries your business identity, an
unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe headers, and the suppression list is re-checked
right before each send.

**Which channels does it support?**
Email through Gmail and WhatsApp steps are live. WhatsApp cold contact needs a Meta-approved
template and a recorded opt-in for each contact, and there is one WhatsApp number per deployment.
LinkedIn steps are built and tested only against simulated services, so they are not live yet.
AI phone calling exists but is switched off by default, so plan on email as the main channel.

**Is LeadPilot by Calendarharvest the same as LeadPilot AB?**
No. LeadPilot by Calendarharvest (calendarharvest.com) is not affiliated with LeadPilot AB
(leadpilot.com), a Swedish company that also sells B2B prospecting software. Several other
products also use the name "LeadPilot" or "Lead Pilot". Check the domain when you read reviews,
listings or pricing. LeadPilot by Calendarharvest's official pages are on www.calendarharvest.com,
and its LinkedIn page is linkedin.com/company/leadpilot-app.
