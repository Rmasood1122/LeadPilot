# Vectorae use cases

Vectorae by Calendarharvest is an AI outbound sales tool for agencies and B2B service
firms: it researches your market, finds and verifies business contacts, writes one-to-one
emails, sends on a schedule you approve each day, and books meetings. Vectorae is the
approve-first AI outbound system for founder-led agencies and B2B service firms (5–50
people) who want booked sales meetings from a plain description of what they sell,
without building lead lists, writing sequences or hiring an SDR.

The ten use cases below follow Vectorae's own customer research (`docs/ICP.md`), which
ranks agencies first, then commercial cleaning, consultancies, staffing and commercial
HVAC/facilities. Use cases 2, 8, 9 and 10 are adjacent fits and say so.

## 1. How a Marketing Agency Founder Uses Vectorae to Keep the Pipeline Moving During Busy Months

**Problem.** In an agency of 5 to 25 people, the founder is usually both the best
salesperson and the person clients ask for. When a big project lands, prospecting stops.
Six weeks later the project ends and there's nothing behind it, so the agency swings between
"too busy to sell" and "selling in a panic".

Agency founders know outbound works, because they often run it for clients. What they lack
is the hours to research a market, build a list, write good first emails and keep follow-ups
going while delivering client work. Templated cold email gets ignored, and hiring a
salesperson is a big bet for a small agency.

**Solution with Vectorae.** The founder describes the agency's offer, for example "Webflow
sites and SEO for B2B SaaS companies with 20–200 employees", and adds two or three past
clients and how they were won. Vectorae runs its 72-step research pipeline and produces a
strategy covering ideal customer, competitors, messaging and objections. The strategy must
pass 10 checks before anything else happens.

Vectorae then sources contacts at matching companies, verifies their email addresses and
writes each first email for one person. Claims in the email are checked. The founder's
daily task is to look at what's queued and approve it, from the web app or the Android app.

Sending ramps up gradually from the founder's own Gmail (10 on the first day, up to 100 a
day), so the agency's domain isn't put at risk by a sudden spike. Replies are classified,
and the sequence stops when a person replies (an out-of-office reply only pauses it). Interested prospects book through the
founder's Vectorae booking page.

**Results.** What to measure: meetings booked per month, reply rate, and founder hours per
week spent on new business, before and after. `[FILL IN — own-use or customer number + date +
how measured]`

**Comparison.** Unlike Apollo, where the agency gets a contact database and a sequencer and
still builds the list and writes the sequence itself, Vectorae does the research and writing
and leaves the founder one approval step.

## 2. How an IT Services (MSP) Owner Uses Vectorae to Reach Office Managers and Operations Leads

*Adjacent fit: a B2B service firm that sells to other businesses. Not separately researched
in the ICP.*

**Problem.** Small managed-service providers usually grow through referrals and a few
local relationships. When a big client churns or is acquired, there's no outbound engine
to replace the revenue, and the owner is also the senior engineer.

Generic MSP marketing ("we keep your IT running") sounds the same as every competitor, and
buyers at 20–100 person companies ignore it. The owner knows the pitch has to name a
specific risk, such as compliance, a recent breach in the sector, or an office move, but has
no time to research each prospect.

**Solution with Vectorae.** The owner describes the service and the area it covers.
Vectorae's research identifies the best-fit segments and the messages most likely to land
with each, and verification checks the strategy for unsupported claims and compliance
problems before outreach starts.

Contacts are sourced at matching companies and email-verified. Each email is written to
the person and company, using company news where it's available. Emails are checked so they
don't claim things Vectorae can't support.

The owner approves sending each morning. Replies come back classified (interested, question, objection, not
interested, unsubscribe request, out of office), so the owner only handles the conversations
that matter. An unsubscribe request suppresses the person immediately.

**Results.** What to measure: first meetings per month and cost per meeting against the
monthly plan or the $179 pay-per-meeting price. `[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike a lead-generation agency on a monthly retainer, Vectorae sends from
the owner's own Gmail in their own name, and the owner approves every day's sending.

## 3. How a Fractional Executive or Consultant Uses Vectorae to Book Discovery Calls

**Problem.** A fractional CFO, CMO or independent consultant sells their own time. Every
hour spent prospecting is an hour not billed, so prospecting happens in bursts between
engagements, which is exactly when pipeline is needed most and has had least attention.

LinkedIn posting builds awareness slowly. Cold outreach done by hand takes research per
prospect to sound credible, and a consultant's reputation suffers if the outreach reads
like spam.

**Solution with Vectorae.** The consultant describes their offer and past engagements.
With past clients provided, research runs the 72-step pipeline and anchors the strategy in
patterns from real wins. Without them, it runs 144 steps.

Each email is written for one decision-maker, and claims are checked. That matters for a
consultant, whose credibility is the product. Volume stays low by design.

Prospects book directly on the consultant's Vectorae booking page. Double booking across
pages is prevented, and confirmation emails show times in each person's time zone.

**Results.** What to measure: discovery calls per month, and engagements won from them.
`[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike Instantly or Smartlead, which are built to send at volume from many
mailboxes, Vectorae caps each mailbox at 100 emails a day by default and writes each email
for one person.

## 4. How a Staffing Agency Owner Uses Vectorae to Open New Client Accounts

**Problem.** Staffing agencies get most new clients through relationships. Vectorae's ICP
research cites 41% from referrals and 44% from word of mouth (`docs/ICP.md` §2). When
relationships plateau, agencies turn to outbound, and the ICP research notes that
commercial staffing prospects take eight to ten touches over four to six weeks.

That multi-touch effort is what owners know they can't keep up with consistently alongside
recruiting work. Staffing buyers are also heavily prospected, so generic outreach gets
ignored.

**Solution with Vectorae.** The owner describes the roles and industries the agency
fills. Research produces a strategy with segments, messaging and objection handling, and
verification checks it.

Contacts at matching companies are sourced and verified. Emails are written one to one,
and follow-ups run as a sequence that stops automatically when a person replies.

**Honest limits.** Vectorae does not target by live hiring signals or VMS/preferred-supplier
status, which some staffing-specific tools do (ICP §1, rank 4). Signal Radar, a signal-based
feature, is built but switched off by default. LinkedIn steps are not live.

**Results.** What to measure: new client meetings per month and meeting-to-job-order rate.
`[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike staffing-specific prospecting tools that target hiring signals,
Vectorae gives you a researched strategy and one-to-one emails, but no hiring-signal
targeting today.

## 5. How a Commercial Cleaning Company Uses Vectorae to Win Office and Property-Manager Contracts

**Problem.** Commercial cleaning companies that have grown past the owner-and-crew stage
(roughly 10–50 staff) live on recurring contracts. Losing one large account creates a
revenue hole that referrals and bids fill slowly.

The buyers are property managers, office managers and facilities leads at nearby businesses.
They're reachable, but the owner has no time to find them, check emails and write to each
one.

**Solution with Vectorae.** Local business mode targets a service area: enter a city or
postcode and a radius, plus what you offer business buyers. Research and messaging focus on
business buyers in that area. The lead search uses the city or postcode; the radius guides
research and messaging but isn't a hard filter.

Messages may say you're based in or serve the area. They never claim a distance or walking
time to the prospect, because Vectorae doesn't know where the prospect's office is.

The owner approves sending daily, and interested prospects book a site walk-through on the
owner's booking page.

**Results.** What to measure: walk-throughs booked per month and contracts signed.
`[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike buying a generic local business list and emailing it (which
Vectorae doesn't allow, since leads can't be imported), Vectorae sources and verifies each
contact and writes to them individually.

## 6. How a Commercial HVAC or Fire Protection Company Uses Vectorae to Find Facilities Buyers

**Problem.** Most HVAC firms are small and residential. Vectorae's ICP notes the majority
are owner-operated with fewer than five employees. The commercial firms in the 5–50 range
sell maintenance contracts to facilities managers, and those buyers are hard to reach
through ads aimed at homeowners.

Fire protection and HVAC maintenance are recurring, compliance-driven purchases, so the
right message at the right time (inspection cycles, a new building, a facilities change)
matters more than volume.

**Solution with Vectorae.** The owner describes the service and area. Research builds a
strategy for commercial buyers, such as property managers, facilities directors and office
operations, and verification checks it, including the compliance check.

Contacts are sourced and verified, and each email is written one to one. WhatsApp steps
are available on plans that include them, but cold WhatsApp needs a Meta-approved template
and a recorded opt-in, so most cold outreach stays on email.

**Results.** What to measure: facilities meetings and quotes issued per month. `[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike home-services lead marketplaces, which sell consumer leads, Vectorae
only finds people at companies. It's the wrong tool for homeowner leads.

**Confidence note.** Vectorae's ICP ranks this segment lowest-confidence, because market
data doesn't separate commercial from residential HVAC.

## 7. How an Accounting or Immigration Firm Partner Uses Vectorae to Reach Business Clients

*ICP rank 3, marked "assumption, not yet validated" in `docs/ICP.md`.*

**Problem.** Small accounting and immigration practices that serve businesses (bookkeeping,
fractional controller work, employer-sponsored visas) depend on referrals. Partners bill
hours and rarely have time for structured new-business work.

These buyers are also careful about tone. Hyped or inaccurate outreach damages a
professional firm's reputation.

**Solution with Vectorae.** The firm describes its business-facing services. Research
identifies segments, such as companies hiring internationally or growing past the
bookkeeping stage, and verification checks the strategy, including a legal and compliance
check.

Claim verification keeps emails from stating things the firm can't support. Vectorae
doesn't give legal advice: before launch it asks the user to confirm they may lawfully
contact each person, and it records that confirmation.

**Results.** What to measure: consultations booked per month. `[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike a generic email marketing tool, Vectorae sources the business
contacts itself, writes one-to-one emails and applies suppression and unsubscribe rules
automatically.

## 8. How a Restaurant or Caterer Uses Vectorae to Win Corporate Catering Accounts

*Local business mode. Business buyers only.*

**Problem.** A restaurant or caterer with spare capacity on weekday lunches wants recurring
corporate orders: team lunches, events and office catering. Walking in with flyers doesn't
scale, and consumer marketing tools reach diners, not office managers.

**Solution with Vectorae.** On the first step of a new strategy, the owner answers "Is
this a local business?" with "restaurant, café or caterer", enters the service area, and
describes the corporate offer. Vectorae researches and writes for business buyers nearby,
such as office managers and HR and events leads.

Vectorae never finds or cold-messages members of the public. A separate customer win-back
feature, for messaging a business's own opted-in customers, is switched off by default
pending legal review.

**Results.** What to measure: corporate tastings or orders per month. `[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike consumer marketing platforms, Vectorae only contacts people at
companies, about business purchases.

## 9. How an Early-Stage B2B SaaS Founder Uses Vectorae to Book First Demos

*Adjacent, partial fit. Vectorae is designed for service firms. SaaS founders with a
clear B2B buyer can use it, but some needs (high volume, product-led signals, LinkedIn)
are not covered today.*

**Problem.** Before the first sales hire, the founder does every demo. Early outbound is
about learning which segment responds, not volume, but founders rarely have time to research
segments properly.

**Solution with Vectorae.** The founder describes the product. With no past customers,
research runs the 144-step pipeline, which is built for zero-history products. The strategy
is verified, contacts are sourced and checked, and one-to-one emails go out after daily
approval.

The learning loop stores outcomes and promotes A/B winners, so later strategies draw on what
worked.

**Results.** What to measure: demos per month, and which segment converts. `[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike Clay, which gives growth teams a flexible enrichment workbench to
build their own workflows, Vectorae is an end-to-end flow with fewer knobs. That's better
for a founder short on time, worse for a team that wants custom data pipelines.

## 10. How a Clinic Uses Vectorae to Offer Occupational Health Services to Local Employers

*Local business mode. Business-to-business only.*

**Problem.** Clinics that offer occupational health, screenings or staff wellness sell those
services to employers. They need to reach HR and operations leads at nearby companies, while
staying well clear of patient data.

**Solution with Vectorae.** In local business mode, choose "clinic, hospital or healthcare
practice". Vectorae researches employers, schools, sports clubs, referring practices and
insurers in the area, and writes about services for an organisation.

Outreach never uses or mentions patient information. Healthcare businesses cannot use the
customer win-back feature at all, because their customer list is a patient list.

**Results.** What to measure: employer meetings per month. `[FILL IN]`

**Comparison.** Unlike patient-marketing tools, Vectorae is for selling services to
organisations, not for contacting patients.
