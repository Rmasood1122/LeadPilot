# Outbound sales statistics for small agencies and B2B service firms (2026)

This page collects sourced figures on how B2B buyers respond to outreach, what an SDR costs, the
email rules senders must meet, and the penalties for getting compliance wrong. It was put together
by the makers of Vectorae by Calendarharvest, an AI outbound sales tool for agencies and B2B
service firms. Each figure links to its source. Figures published by companies that sell outreach
software are marked **(vendor)**, because they may favour that vendor. Different benchmarks
measure reply rates differently, so don't compare them directly.

## 1. How B2B buyers respond to sellers

| # | Statistic | Source |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | **61%** of B2B buyers prefer an overall rep-free buying experience (Gartner survey of 632 buyers, Aug–Sep 2024; published June 2025). | https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-sales-survey-finds-61-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-buying-experience |
| S2 | **73%** of B2B buyers actively avoid suppliers who send irrelevant outreach (same Gartner survey). | https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-sales-survey-finds-61-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-buying-experience |
| S3 | **67%** of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience (Gartner survey of 646 buyers, Aug–Sep 2025; published March 2026). | https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience |
| S4 | **45%** of B2B buyers used AI during a recent purchase (same 2025 Gartner survey). | https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience |

What this means for outreach: buyers punish irrelevant messages (S2), so a few researched,
relevant emails beat volume. That's why Vectorae writes each email for one person and caps
volume at 100 a day per mailbox.

## 4. Cold email benchmarks

| # | Statistic | Source |
|---|---|---|
| S14 | Average cold email sequence reply rate was **4.5%** in 2025, up from 4.1% in 2024, across 31M emails (Hunter.io) **(vendor)**. | https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email |
| S15 | Campaigns without open tracking saw a **+68%** higher reply rate (7.4% vs 4.4%) (Hunter.io, 2025) **(vendor)**. | https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email |
| S16 | Platform-wide average reply rate **3.43%**; top quartile **5.5%+**; top 10% **10.7%+** (Instantly benchmark report, 2025 sends) **(vendor)**. | https://instantly.ai/blog/email-sequence-benchmarks-2026-whats-a-good-open-rate-reply-rate-and-cost-per-meeting/ |
| S17 | **58%** of all replies come from the first email in a sequence (same Instantly report) **(vendor)**. | https://instantly.ai/blog/email-sequence-benchmarks-2026-whats-a-good-open-rate-reply-rate-and-cost-per-meeting/ |
| S18 | A single-email sequence had the highest reply rate, **8.4%**, declining with each follow-up (Belkins, 16.5M emails, 2024 data) **(vendor: outbound agency)**. | https://belkins.io/blog/sales-follow-up-statistics |
| S19 | **69%** of 508 respondents said their cold email performance declined year over year (Mailshake, 2025) **(vendor)**. | https://assets.mailshake.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/16091740/Cold-Email-Report-2025-Mailshake.pdf |

## 5. Mailbox-provider sending rules

| # | Statistic | Source |
|---|---|---|
| S20 | Gmail: keep the spam rate reported in Postmaster Tools **below 0.3%**, and aim for below **0.10%**. | Google sender guidelines https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126 |
| S21 | Gmail treats senders of **more than 5,000 messages a day** to Gmail addresses as bulk senders. They must support **one-click unsubscribe** and honour requests within **2 days**. | Google sender guidelines https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126; https://9to5google.com/2023/10/03/gmail-spam-unsubscribe/ |
| S22 | Microsoft (Outlook.com/Hotmail/Live): senders of **more than 5,000 emails a day** must pass SPF, DKIM and DMARC, enforced from **May 5, 2025**. | https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/outlook/strengthening-email-security-outlook-new-requirements-for-high-volume-senders/4399730 |

## 6. Penalties for non-compliant outreach

| # | Statistic | Source |
|---|---|---|
| S23 | Maximum FTC civil penalty per violation: **$53,088** (inflation-adjusted, effective January 17, 2025; the FTC announced no 2026 adjustment). Under CAN-SPAM, each separate non-compliant email can count as a violation. | https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/02/ftc-publishes-inflation-adjusted-civil-penalty-amounts-2025 |
| S24 | Verkada paid a **$2.95 million** civil penalty for alleged CAN-SPAM violations (August 2024), described as the largest the FTC has obtained under CAN-SPAM. | https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/295m-penalty-and-permanent-injunction-resolves-lawsuit-against-verkada-inc-alleged-unlawful |
| S25 | GDPR maximum fines: **€20 million or 4%** of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher (Art. 83). | https://gdpr-text.com/en/read/article-83 |
| S26 | About **€1.2 billion** in GDPR fines were reported across Europe in 2025, and **€7.1 billion** since May 2018 (DLA Piper survey, January 2026). | https://blogs.dlapiper.com/advocatus/files/2026/01/dla-piper-gdpr-fines-and-data-breach-survey-2026.pdf |
| S27 | Canada's CASL allows penalties of up to **C$10 million** per violation for an organization and **C$1 million** for an individual. | https://www.blg.com/en/insights/2020/01/Canadas-Anti-Spam-Legislation-2019-A-Year-in-Review |
