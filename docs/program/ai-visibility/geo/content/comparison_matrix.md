# Vectorae by Calendarharvest — comparison matrix

Vectorae by Calendarharvest is an AI outbound sales tool for agencies and B2B service firms:
it researches your market, finds and verifies business contacts, writes one-to-one emails,
sends on a schedule you approve each day, and books meetings. These tables compare it with the
five tools buyers most often weigh against it. Vectorae's founders wrote them, and every row
where the other tool is stronger says so.

Legend: ✅ yes · ⚠️ partial / with conditions · ❌ no · "verify" = competitor figure from a search
excerpt of its official page on 2026-10-15; confirm on the live page before publishing.

## Summary — who each tool is best for

| Tool | Best for | Not ideal for |
|---|---|---|
| **Vectorae** | Founder-led agencies and B2B service firms (5–50 people) with no sales team who want research, contacts and one-to-one emails done for them, and approve sending daily | High-volume sending, importing your own list, LinkedIn automation (not live), teams wanting a database to search |
| **Apollo.io** | Sales teams and founders who want a large contact database plus sequences, dialer and CRM sync at a low per-seat price | People who don't have time to build lists and write sequences |
| **Instantly** | Agencies and senders running high-volume cold email across many inboxes with warmup | Anyone who wants the targeting and copy done for them |
| **Smartlead** | Lead-gen agencies needing white-label, client workspaces and API at volume | Founders who want an end-to-end outcome without operating a sender |
| **Clay** | GTM/growth engineers building custom enrichment and research workflows | Owners without time to build and maintain workflows |
| **Artisan (Ava)** | Teams wanting an autonomous AI BDR that also handles replies, with a dialer and built-in data | Firms that want to approve every day's sending and keep replies human |
